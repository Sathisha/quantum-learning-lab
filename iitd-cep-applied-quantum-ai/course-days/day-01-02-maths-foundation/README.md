# Course Days 1 & 2: Maths foundation quiz (Sat 17 – Sun 18 Oct 2026)

Worked solutions to the 20-question multiple-choice quiz from the IIT Delhi CEP maths foundation sessions.

**Topics:** complex numbers; vector spaces over ℂ; inner products, norms, orthonormal bases; linear maps as matrices.
