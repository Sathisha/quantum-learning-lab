## Modules

| Module | Topics | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1 Foundations | Linear algebra, qubits, entanglement, gates | Oct–Nov |
| M2 Core algorithms | Deutsch–Jozsa, QFT, phase estimation, Shor, Grover | Nov–Dec |
| M3 Circuits & hardware | Gates, NISQ, devices | Oct–Jan |
| M4 Complexity & QML | BQP/QMA, quantum walks, quantum ML | Feb–Mar |
| M5 Crypto & QEC | QKD, post-quantum crypto, fault tolerance | Jan–Apr |

**Capstones:** MC1 circuit harness and noise characterisation · MC2 hardware-aware compilation · MC3 QAOA starter and quantum-kernel starter · MC4 post-quantum cryptography (standalone) · Final: end-to-end hybrid application. MC1–MC3 build up to the final project.

## Books

- **Start here:** N. D. Mermin, *Quantum Computer Science*, Cambridge 2007. Short, written for computer scientists, no physics assumed.
- **Main reference:** Nielsen & Chuang, *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information*, Cambridge 2010. Dense; its chapters map to the modules.
